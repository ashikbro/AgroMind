# Workflow Example: Smallholder Vegetable Farm

## Objective
Detect early disease risk in tomato field and optimize treatment timing.

## Step-by-step
1. Login and create/update farm profile
2. Upload 2-3 clear images of symptomatic leaves
3. Add symptom notes (spots, yellowing, spread pattern)
4. Submit disease analysis request
5. Review:
   - Primary diagnosis
   - Confidence score
   - Immediate and prevention recommendations
6. Apply treatment and log a follow-up after 3-5 days
7. Monitor diagnosis history and analytics trend for recurrence risk

## Expected Outcome
- Faster treatment decision
- Lower spread risk
- Better yield stability through early intervention
